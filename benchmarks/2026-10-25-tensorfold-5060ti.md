# TensorFold on the RTX 5060 Ti eGPU: first CUDA runtime, measured (2026-10-25)

Host: 16-inch M1 Max laptop, eGPU kernel `7.1.12-2-20261013.20-egpu-ARCH`,
RTX 5060 Ti dock chained on the B60's Sonnet enclosure (one Thunderbolt
tunnel). TensorFold 1.1.4 native release (aarch64), CUDA backend, drivers
595.112.07 and 615.78.08 (both loaded from staged userspace; see the
[NVIDIA window](../cards/rtx-5060-ti/setup.md)).

## What was measured

TensorFold 2B smoke: Qwen3.5-2B MLX 4-bit checkpoint (pinned sha
`713fe7e5d3c3965f7106b0d0ee17615f7869c23c8d327996df8c1196fbcf07d5`),
fully on the card (2.2 GiB peak VRAM), loopback only, cold cache,
31-token prompt, 128 generated tokens, n=1 per driver:

| Driver | TTFT | Decode | Note |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 595.112.07 | 0.0197 s | 260.8 tok/s | identical |
| 615.78.08 | 0.0197 s | 260.8 tok/s | identical |

llama.cpp CUDA on the same card, same boot, same driver pair
(commit `65840ed5`, CUDA 13.4, n=1):

| Model | fa | pp512 | tg128 | 595 vs 615 |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| Qwen3.8-27B Q4_0 | 1 | 933.8 / 934.1 | 24.6 / 24.6 | identical |
| Gemma-4-12B Q4_0 | 1 | 2033.8 / 2030.9 | 49.8 / 49.7 | identical |

Same-model-class comparison (Qwen3.5-2B, same card, driver 615):

| Runtime | Format | Decode | TTFT |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| TensorFold 1.1.4 | MLX 4-bit | 260.8 tok/s | 0.020 s |
| llama.cpp CUDA | GGUF Q4_K_M | 171.8 tok/s | — |

TensorFold is 1.5x llama.cpp CUDA on decode for this 2B class on this card.

## Caveats, stated plainly

- The TensorFold and llama.cpp numbers are NOT the same measurement:
  TensorFold's decode rate is stream delivery over its loopback API;
  llama-bench is a raw compute benchmark. The numbers are comparable as
  user-facing rates, not as identical workloads.
- The quantization formats differ: MLX 4-bit (TensorFold's native) vs
  GGUF Q4_K_M (llama.cpp's). Same model, same card, same driver; the
  formats are not interchangeable.
- n=1 per driver per model; one boot per driver profile; stock power
  and clocks throughout; no Xid lines in either boot.
- TensorFold model support on this release: the 2B MLX smoke fits and
  runs. No supported 16-GiB NVFP4 or EXL3 checkpoint exists in this
  release; unsupported formats are not OOM-tested (see the model audit).

## Honesty box

- One host, one card, one cable topology. No cross-machine claim.
- The 615.78.08 driver loaded from staged userspace (versioned directory,
  no system-wide install); 595 remains available as a one-command
  fallback (`NV_FALLBACK_595=1`).
- The TensorFold stream rate includes HTTP framing; a matched
  llama-server comparison with exposed timings is pending.

Receipts: token + output SHA256 per run, health JSON, nvidia-smi
snapshots, full kernel journals (zero Xid) — in the window receipt
