# RTX 5060 Ti on a 16-inch M1 Max MacBook Pro: NVIDIA open + CUDA/Vulkan, 2026-10-23

MEASURED: the NVIDIA open modules 595.112.07 load and initialize on the patched 7.1.12 16K-page eGPU kernel (the 16K fix in `memmgrDeterminePageSize` is the one whose absence kills the load; see the [patch page](../../cards/rtx-5060-ti/patches.md)). `nvidia-smi` reports the card healthy with CUDA 13.2. With the EGL files in place (the measured cause: the ICD's init goes through distribution glvnd `libEGL.so.1` and needs `libEGL_nvidia.so.0` plus the glvnd vendor manifest `10_nvidia.json`; details in [setup](../../cards/rtx-5060-ti/setup.md)), llama.cpp enumerates `Vulkan0 : NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5060 Ti (16311 MiB)`.

The card ran alone on the tunnel (the Intel card's SR-IOV VF BARs consume the window when both are attached).
